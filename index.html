Housing

Where Elmwood Students Live

Five main groups make up the student body, 400 students in total.

Greek Row

60 students

Twelve fraternity and sorority houses lined up along the edge of the quad, right next to the administration building. Porches are rarely empty.

North Hall

80 students

Elmwood's largest first-year dorm, set back near the athletic fields on the far side of campus. About a fifteen-minute walk from the quad.

Off-Campus Commuters

100 students

Students who live off campus and come in for classes, labs, and practice. Mostly seen moving between the parking structures and their next building.

Athletics Dorm

80 students

Home to Elmwood's varsity athletes, attached to the fieldhouse. Practice, travel, and study hall keep the schedule tight.

International House

80 students

A shared dorm for international and exchange students, built around one big common kitchen. Weekend potlucks are a fixture.
